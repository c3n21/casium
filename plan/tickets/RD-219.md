# RD-219 Restyle The Remaining Pages

## Goal
Bring home, agent, and provider onto the new primitives so the app is not half-overhauled.

## Implementation
Purely presentational. `apps/web/app/page.tsx` (60 lines), `app/agent/page.tsx` (665 lines), `app/provider/page.tsx` (235 lines), and the shared `AppShell`, `ApplicationInbox`, `ListingForm`, `MandateStatus`, `RevokeButton`, `WithdrawButton`. The agent page is the large one and the one with the most inline styling; it is also the page a viewer watches during a run, so its progress and result states deserve real components rather than restyled `<p>` tags. Change no behavior, no data flow, and no `data-testid`.

## Done when
- [ ] All four pages render on the new primitives.
- [ ] `pnpm test:e2e` passes with no selector changes.
- [ ] No `style={{` remains anywhere under `apps/web`.

## Verify
- **Tests.** Existing E2E suite, unchanged.
- **Evidence to capture.** Screenshots of all four pages plus a passing stubbed E2E run.

## Notes
- **Failure fallback.** Ship page by page; partial restyling is ugly but not broken.
- **Parallel safety.** Conflicts with RD-214. Land after it.
- **Demo impact.** Moderate — consistency across the pages a viewer clicks through.
