# RD-219 Restyle The Remaining Pages

## Goal
Bring home, agent, and provider onto the new primitives so the app is not half-overhauled.

## Implementation
Purely presentational. `apps/web/app/page.tsx` (60 lines), `app/agent/page.tsx` (665 lines), `app/provider/page.tsx` (235 lines), and the shared `AppShell`, `ApplicationInbox`, `ListingForm`, `MandateStatus`, `RevokeButton`, `WithdrawButton`. The agent page is the large one and the one with the most inline styling; it is also the page a viewer watches during a run, so its progress and result states deserve real components rather than restyled `<p>` tags. Change no behavior, no data flow, and no `data-testid`.

## Scope correction (2026-07-31, user-authorized)

RD-214 already removed every `style={{` and moved these files onto utilities, so two of the
original *Done when* items were satisfied before this ticket started. What remains is
replacing the **legacy semantic classes** RD-214 deliberately kept alive — 49 usages across
the 9 owned files, 18 of them on the agent page.

That needs variants the primitives do not have: `Alert` ships only `default`/`destructive`
but the app uses `.alert success|warn|error`, and `Badge` lacks `success`/`warn`/`info`.
`alert.tsx` and `badge.tsx` were therefore added to `owns:`. The new variants must reproduce
the legacy colours in `globals.css` exactly, because the agreed bar is *near-identical*
rendering. Do **not** delete the legacy rules from `globals.css`: `/renter` and `/landlord`
still use them and belong to other tickets.

## Done when
- [ ] All four pages render on the new primitives.
- [ ] No legacy semantic class (`card`, `alert`, `badge`, `step-card`, `evidence-panel`,
      `table-shell`, `listing-option`, `listing-grid`, `pipeline`, `hero-panel`) remains in
      the 9 owned files. Layout classes (`page`, `stack`, `cluster`, `split`, `grid`) may
      stay — no primitive replaces them.
- [ ] `pnpm test:e2e` passes with no selector changes.
- [ ] No `style={{` remains anywhere under `apps/web`.

## Verify
- **Tests.** Existing E2E suite, unchanged.
- **Evidence to capture.** Screenshots of all four pages plus a passing stubbed E2E run.
