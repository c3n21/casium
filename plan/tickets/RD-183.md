# RD-183 Role-Scoped Application Inbox

## Goal
Stop showing the landlord a provider's controls, so each dashboard states one role's job.

## Implementation
`ApplicationInbox` is rendered identically on `/provider` and `/landlord`, and its only interactive control is the *Verify Sui receipt* form that calls `POST /applications/:id/verify` (`ApplicationInbox.tsx:35-58,84-116`). Receipt verification is the provider's job — it is what moves an application to `accepted`. Add a required `role: "provider" \| "landlord"` prop and render the verify block only for `provider`. For `landlord`, render the receipt link, the status badge, and (after RD-184) the packet panel. Do not fork the component into two files: the shared parts are most of it, and two copies will drift. Keep every existing `data-testid` stable for the `provider` role so the E2E specs do not churn; add landlord-specific test IDs rather than renaming.

## Done when
- [ ] `/landlord` shows no verify inputs and no verify button.
- [ ] `/provider` is unchanged pixel-for-pixel.
- [ ] The `role` prop is required, so a future third caller cannot default into the wrong surface.

## Verify
- **Tests.** Component-level or stubbed-tier E2E: assert `verify-receipt-summary` is present on `/provider` and absent on `/landlord`.
- **Evidence to capture.** Stubbed-tier E2E run; both dashboards screenshotted.
