# RD-183 — evidence

Status: **DONE**

2026-07-31. The role scoping itself was already implemented in the tree and unmarked: `ApplicationInbox`
takes a required `role: "provider" | "landlord"` (`ApplicationInbox.tsx:35`, no default), renders the
verify-receipt block only under `role === "provider"` (line 106) and the
`landlord-awaiting-receipt` line only under `role === "landlord"` (line 138), and both dashboards
already passed the prop. What was missing was the one thing that made it provable.

`apps/web/app/landlord/page.tsx` filters applications by `landlordSuiAddress` against the connected
wallet. With no wallet the stubbed tier stood in `PUBLISHER_ADDRESS` — the **provider's** address —
so the filter matched nothing, the inbox always rendered its empty state, and
`specs/provider-dashboard.spec.ts:82` *"keeps receipt verification controls scoped to the provider
role"* failed on its first assertion, never reaching the two assertions that actually test the role
split. Fixed by standing in `LANDLORD_ADDRESS`, imported from `@casium/contracts-config` rather than
restated as a literal (`node scripts/check-object-ids.mjs` still reports its 3 pre-existing findings,
none new).

## Done-when, against evidence

- **`/landlord` shows no verify inputs or button.** `pnpm test:e2e` now exercises the assertions that
  were previously unreachable: `verify-receipt-summary` has count 0 on `/landlord`, and
  `landlord-awaiting-receipt` contains *"Awaiting on-chain receipt verification by the provider."*

  byte-identical for `/`, `/renter`, `/provider` and `/agent`; only `/landlord` differs, which is the
  intended blast radius.
- **`role` is required**, so a third caller cannot default into the wrong surface.
